Spend coins dropped by eliminated BLUBs. Purchased weapons are yours for the rest of this run. Number keys 1–5 switch between weapons you own.
Tip: Scatter Blaster fires a cone of pellets. Triple Burst fires three rounds at once. Hold left click with the Rail Blaster to charge it, then release to chain a beam through nearby visible BLUBs. Beat a boss every five waves to unlock ARC CORE OVERDRIVE.
